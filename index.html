    Privacy Notice

    What we collect. Your name, email, portfolio link, the images you upload, your answers to the three checkboxes, and the date and time you sent them.

    Why. Only to review your design and to contact you about it.

    Where it goes. Submissions are sent to artists@temperedtees.com and forwarded to a private Gmail account (hosted by Google) run by the team that operates Tempered Tees. Only that team reads them. We use your name, email and files only to review your work and reply to you, and we never sell or share them.

    How long. If we don't select your work, we delete it within 12 months. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we delete it from all of our inboxes and storage, including trash, within 30 days of your request.

    No AI training. We never use your submissions to train AI.

    18+ only. This page is for people 18 and older. If we learn someone under 18 sent a design, we delete it.
